The minimum New Mexico requires

New Mexico requires at least 25/50/10 in liability: $25,000 for injury to one person, $50,000 per accident, and $10,000 for property damage. That is the floor for driving legally in Los Lunas — it is not the same thing as being covered.

The gap is worth understanding. $10,000 of property damage does not replace a late-model truck, and the difference in premium between the state minimum and limits that would actually hold up is usually far smaller than people expect. Ask us to quote both and compare the two numbers rather than assuming.
