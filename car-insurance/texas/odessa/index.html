The minimum Texas requires

Texas requires at least 30/60/25 in liability: $30,000 for injury to one person, $60,000 per accident, and $25,000 for property damage. That is the floor for driving legally in Odessa — it is not the same thing as being covered.

The gap is worth understanding. $25,000 of property damage does not replace a late-model truck, and the difference in premium between the state minimum and limits that would actually hold up is usually far smaller than people expect. Ask us to quote both and compare the two numbers rather than assuming.

Work trucks and highway miles

Around Odessa a lot of vehicles are not commuters — they are trucks running to a site and back, sometimes on a company's business and sometimes on yours. That distinction decides which policy you need, and getting it wrong is how a claim gets denied. If the truck in your driveway does any work, say so and we will quote it properly.

Long highway mileage moves the price too. Annual miles is one of the few questions where the honest answer usually helps you.
