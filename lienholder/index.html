Before you send

1
Send it from your institution’s domain

A declarations page lists a customer’s vehicles, drivers and home address. We release it to a lienholder with a recorded interest in the vehicle, and a request arriving from a work address at the institution named on it is the first thing that makes that verifiable. Requests from personal email accounts get a phone call before anything is sent.

2
Give us the exact lienholder wording

"First National Bank" and "First National Bank ISAOA/ATIMA, PO Box 1234" are not the same line to a carrier, and the second one is what your own system will accept back. Nearly every rejected document we see is this and nothing else.

3
Last six of the VIN, not the full one

Six digits identifies the vehicle unambiguously against a policy. There is no reason for a full VIN to travel by email for this, so we do not ask for one.

4
What we will not send

Driver license numbers, dates of birth, payment details and prior claim history do not go out with a lienholder request. If your underwriting genuinely requires something in that list, call us and we will work out the right way to handle it.

Common lender questions

How quickly will we get it?

Same business day for anything requested before 4pm Mountain, Monday to Friday. Adding a lienholder to a policy can take longer — the endorsement goes to the carrier and we send you confirmation once it is issued, usually 24 to 72 hours.

Can you add us as lienholder without the customer?

Yes. Adding a lienholder or loss payee is a routine endorsement and does not need the customer to sign anything. We do notify them, because a change to their policy should never be a surprise.

The coverage does not meet our requirements.

Tell us what the loan requires — deductible caps, minimum limits, comprehensive and collision both in force — and we will take it to the customer and to the carrier. That is faster than force-placing and it is cheaper for everybody.

Do you charge for any of this?

No. Declarations pages, evidence of insurance, verifications and lienholder endorsements are part of servicing the policy.

Can we set up automatic renewal notices?

Yes. Ask in the notes and we will add your institution to the policy so renewal documents go out to you each term without anyone having to ask.

We think the policy has cancelled.

Send the request anyway and say so. We will tell you the actual status the same day, and if it has lapsed we will already be working on reinstating it before your force-placed notice goes out.
