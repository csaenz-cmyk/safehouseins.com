If your policy has already cancelled, do not pay online first.

A payment made after a cancellation date does not always put the policy back in force, and some carriers will keep the money against a balance while you are driving uninsured. Call us before you pay and we will tell you what the carrier actually needs — a reinstatement, a new policy, or just the payment.

Safe House never takes card or bank details on this website, by text, or by email. If somebody contacts you asking for them in our name, it is not us.
