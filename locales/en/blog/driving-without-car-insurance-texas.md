Texas generally requires drivers to maintain proof of financial responsibility. Most drivers meet this obligation with auto liability insurance. Driving without the required financial responsibility can lead to legal and financial consequences.

## What are the minimum insurance limits?

If you use liability insurance to meet the requirement, the standard Texas minimum is **30/60/25**: $30,000 bodily injury per person, $60,000 per accident, and $25,000 property damage per accident. Other legally recognized ways of establishing financial responsibility may exist for eligible individuals.

## What penalties could apply?

Under Texas Transportation Code Section 601.191, a first conviction for operating a motor vehicle without the required financial responsibility generally carries a fine of **$175 to $350**, subject to statutory exceptions. A subsequent conviction generally carries a fine of **$350 to $1,000** and can involve additional consequences under applicable law. The circumstances and court determinations matter.

Even apart from penalties, causing an accident without insurance can create substantial personal financial exposure for covered-type losses that you must pay yourself.

## What if you have a policy but cannot show the card?

Having active insurance and being unable to show proof are not necessarily the same issue. If you receive a citation, follow the court's instructions. Texas law includes circumstances in which proof that qualifying coverage was in effect at the relevant time may affect the charge.

## What if your policy lapsed?

Do not assume that a missed payment leaves coverage active. Contact the insurer or agent promptly to confirm your actual policy status and whether reinstatement or a new policy is possible. Do not drive without satisfying the legal requirements.

## Why proof of insurance and active insurance are not identical

An insurance card shows information about a policy, but a card saved on your phone may not reflect a later cancellation or lapse. Likewise, not having the card immediately available does not necessarily mean no qualifying policy exists. What matters is the legal and factual situation at the relevant time. If your policy was active when you were cited, follow the court's process for providing evidence.

## What happens if an uninsured driver causes an accident?

Apart from a traffic citation, the person who causes a crash may be responsible for injuries and property damage. Without applicable liability coverage, those expenses can become a personal financial obligation. The amount and legal responsibility depend on the facts and applicable law. Even a relatively ordinary crash can involve repair bills, towing, medical treatment, or other losses.

Having only the minimum coverage also does not mean all potential losses are paid: liability limits cap the insurer's obligation for covered claims. Drivers should understand those limits before a loss occurs.

## Steps to take if you discover a cancellation

1. Stop assuming the previous insurance card proves you are covered.
2. Contact the insurer or agent to confirm the exact cancellation date and whether coverage can be reinstated.
3. Ask for written evidence of the effective date of any replacement or reinstated policy.
4. If a state requirement such as an SR-22 applies, verify whether additional steps are needed.
5. Do not drive until you have met the applicable financial responsibility and licensing requirements.

## Can a new policy cover an accident from yesterday?

Do not assume a policy purchased today covers a prior loss. Insurance ordinarily applies only as described by its effective dates and terms. Be truthful about when an accident occurred and when coverage began. If there is a dispute about a prior policy, collect the documents and contact the insurer or appropriate authority.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is liability coverage optional in Texas?** Drivers must generally establish financial responsibility; purchasing the required auto liability insurance is the common method.

**Will the first violation always cost exactly $175?** No. The law states a range and contains exceptions, so the court and circumstances matter.

**Can I rely on a quote confirmation as proof of coverage?** A preliminary quote is not the same as an issued, effective policy. Confirm the policy status and start date.

## Why this matters to families with multiple vehicles

One household may have several cars with different drivers and different policy arrangements. A card for one insured vehicle does not automatically establish coverage for every car or every driver. Check the listed vehicles and named insureds, and tell your carrier when a car is added, sold, or regularly used by someone new. Avoid making assumptions based on a prior policy or a different household member's insurance.

## How to prepare for a conversation with an agent

Have the vehicle identification information, driver details, the date you need coverage to begin, and any insurer notices available. Explain whether coverage is currently active, canceled, or uncertain. An agent can explain available next steps, but only the actual insurer's records and issued documents can confirm an active policy. Keep those documents in an accessible place and review them for accuracy.

## One final distinction

The legal requirement to demonstrate financial responsibility, the contractual coverages you purchased, and the ability to pay for a severe loss are related but not identical. Complying with the state minimum addresses one question. Selecting enough protection for your circumstances is another. Neither should be left to assumptions.
