**Mobile home insurance may provide coverage for an eligible manufactured or mobile home, its contents, certain personal liability claims, and additional living expenses after covered damage.** The type of home, installation, age, location, construction details, and policy terms can significantly affect what an insurer offers. It is not safe to assume that [an ordinary site-built homeowners policy](/blog/what-does-homeowners-insurance-cover-texas/) automatically applies.

For Texas residents buying or insuring a manufactured home, the first step is to describe the home accurately—not simply select “house” from a quote menu.

## Mobile home, manufactured home, or modular home?

People often use these terms interchangeably, but insurers and housing authorities may distinguish factory-built homes based on construction standards, age, transportability, installation, and whether the property is permanently affixed to the land. A manufactured home usually is not evaluated exactly like a site-built house. Modular homes may follow a different underwriting path depending on local construction and the carrier.

Prepare the manufacturer, model, year built, serial or identification numbers, dimensions, number of sections, roof type, exterior materials, anchoring, and current condition. If you are unsure what kind of structure you own, review ownership and installation documents before applying.

## What a policy may insure

| Coverage area | What to ask |
|---|---|
| Home or dwelling | What insured structure is described, at what limit and valuation method? |
| Personal belongings | What household contents qualify, including special limits for valuables? |
| Detached structures | Are a storage shed, steps, awning, or other detached items included or excluded? |
| Personal liability | What covered bodily injury or property damage allegations are addressed? |
| Additional living expenses | What happens if covered damage prevents use of the home? |
| Wind, hail, and flood | Which events are included, excluded, subject to special deductibles, or require separate policies? |

The TDI home-insurance guide describes mobile home insurance as a distinct property product that may address the dwelling, belongings, additional living expenses, and liability. However, actual coverage forms vary, and the policy may handle attachments, skirting, decks, and outbuildings differently.

## The land and the home are different interests

In some parks, a resident owns the manufactured home but rents the lot beneath it. Elsewhere, the homeowner owns both the land and home. Tell the insurer who owns the home, whether it is your primary residence, and whether it is occupied, rented, vacant, or undergoing renovations.

A lot lease can impose responsibilities or insurance requirements, but a park owner's liability or property policy should not be assumed to replace insurance for the resident's manufactured home. A financed home may have lender requirements beyond general state law.

## Replacement cost versus actual cash value

**Replacement cost** and **actual cash value (ACV)** can lead to different claim calculations. Some manufactured homes may have limited valuation options depending on age and condition. Always verify what the proposed policy will value, what depreciation means, and how repairs will be handled.

*Hypothetical arithmetic example:* Suppose a covered loss involves **$15,000 in otherwise eligible repairs** and an applicable **$2,000 deductible**. A simplified calculation under a policy responding on that repair basis would leave **$13,000** before any other limits or conditions. An ACV settlement involving depreciation could be different. This is not a claim projection or valuation advice.

Ask whether the quoted limit realistically reflects eligible replacement or repair costs, rather than just the amount you originally paid for the home. The insured value and market resale value are not always the same.

## Special weather and water questions in Texas

Review wind and hail coverage carefully, including special deductibles and any geographic limitations. Coastal areas may have different windstorm arrangements from El Paso or inland Texas. Flood is generally a **separate risk** from homeowners or mobile home property coverage; ask about flood coverage if the location is exposed.

Water from a burst covered interior pipe, gradual seepage, and rising water from outdoors can produce very different coverage outcomes. Maintenance-related damage, mold, foundation movement, and aging roofs can also be restricted. A policy with a low price may have significant exclusions or depreciation provisions.

## What to gather before requesting a quote

1. Full physical address, plus the lot number if inside a park.
2. Home's year, make, model, serial/label information, and dimensions.
3. Roof, siding, foundation, anchoring, and tie-down details.
4. Current photos, maintenance condition, and any renovations.
5. Whether you own or rent the land and whether a lender has requirements.
6. Occupancy and use: primary residence, tenant-occupied, seasonal, or vacant.
7. Desired dwelling and contents limits, deductible preferences, and other structures.

## A manufactured-home example near El Paso

*Hypothetical scenario:* A homeowner in the El Paso area owns a single-wide manufactured home on a rented lot. The home has a newer roof, but the porch and separate shed were added later. Before comparing policies, the owner identifies the actual manufacturer and year, confirms the home's anchoring, and asks how the proposed contract treats the attached porch and shed. The questions help avoid assuming that every structure on the lot is automatically part of the insured dwelling.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a mobile home be insured if it is older?

Availability depends on the insurer's underwriting rules, condition, construction, and other circumstances. Older homes may face different requirements; no company is required to offer a particular policy.

### Does mobile home insurance cover flood?

Do not assume it does. Flood commonly requires separate coverage. Confirm the policy and any waiting periods before relying on it.

### Is a manufactured home covered by the landowner's insurance?

Not automatically. The home owner and landowner may have separate insured interests, especially where a manufactured home sits on a rented lot.
