2. What we send

We send customer care and account notification messages only. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages. Typical messages include:

  • A payment is due, or a payment did not go through
  • Your policy is at risk of cancellation, and what is needed to keep it active
  • Documents we need from you, or documents that need your signature
  • Confirmation that we received your payment or documents
  • Your policy renewal
  • Replies to questions you text us
  • A quote you asked us for

Sample messages:

Safe House Insurance: Your Progressive policy 12345 needs your signature on disclosures by August 5, 2026 or it will cancel. Call 915-503-1207. Reply STOP to opt out.
Safe House Insurance: We did not receive the monthly payment for your auto policy. To avoid cancellation, please make your payment before August 5, 2026. You can reply to this message or call us at (915) 503-1207. Reply STOP to opt out.
Safe House Insurance: FINAL NOTICE. Your Kemper policy KMP25349856 cancels August 5, 2026. Call 915-503-1207 NOW to save your coverage. Reply STOP to opt out.

3. How you opt in

You give us permission to text you in one of these ways:

  • On our website. You check the box next to “I agree to receive text messages from Safe House Insurance” on a quote or contact form. This box is never checked for you, and you can submit the form without checking it — we will simply call or email you instead.
  • In person or by phone. You give an agent your mobile number and tell them you agree to receive text messages. The agent records your consent, with the date and time, in our system.
  • By texting us first. If you text (915) 594-3777, we will reply.

We keep a record of when, how, and from which number each consent was given.

Your consent is not a condition of buying insurance from us. You can be our client and never receive a text message.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your policy and your account activity. Most clients receive only a few messages per policy term. If a payment is missed, you may receive several messages in the days before the cancellation date.

5. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Safe House Insurance does not charge you for these messages, but your mobile carrier may, depending on your plan. Contact your carrier if you have questions about your plan.

6. How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. We will send one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then we will stop.

We also accept: UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REMOVE — and, in Spanish, ALTO, PARAR, CANCELAR, BAJA.

Opting out of text messages does not cancel your insurance policy and does not stop us from contacting you by phone, mail, or email about your coverage. If we need to reach you about something urgent — a payment that would cancel your policy, for example — we will call you.

To start receiving messages again, reply START (also: UNSTOP, SUBSCRIBE, EMPEZAR, REINICIAR, SUSCRIBIR).
