Our story

Big-tech simple. Hometown human.

Safe House Insurance was built on a simple idea: insurance shouldn't be slow, confusing or impersonal. We pair modern tools — real-time carrier comparison and an effortless online experience — with something the big apps can't offer: real, local, bilingual people who pick up the phone.

As an independent agency, we don't work for one insurance company. We work for you. That means we shop top carriers like Progressive, Geico, Lemonade, Alinsco and Commonwealth to find the coverage and price that fit your life — including drivers and families other agencies turn away.
