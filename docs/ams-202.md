# For the AMS side — a 202 on a complete submission

A real quote from safehouseins.com came back **202**. The panel on the
results page read:

> 202 from the bridge — the AMS saved the lead but could not rate.

That is the website behaving exactly as designed: the lead was saved, no
price was shown, the visitor landed on the agent screen. Nothing on the
site needs changing. The rating itself failed inside the AMS, and only the
AMS knows why.

## What we changed on our side

A 202 used to be reported with a fixed sentence. It now reads the response
body and shows whichever of `reason`, `error`, `message`, `detail` or
`ratingError` is present, falling back to `warnings`, then to the
unrecognised fields themselves. The quote or client id is shown as a
reference. The whole body is also logged and left on `window.__quote202`.

**If the 202 currently has an empty body, please put a reason in it.** One
string is enough. Without it the next screenshot says "the AMS gave no
reason" and we are guessing again.

## What we need from you

1. **The reason this specific quote failed.** The lead is in the system —
   the run should be in your logs with whatever TurboRater returned.
2. **Confirmation that every value below is in the enum you map to.** A
   single out-of-enum value takes down the whole quote, and these are the
   only strings the form can produce. They are sent verbatim, unmapped.

## Everything the form can send

Exact strings. Nothing is renamed between the browser and the bridge.

**occupation** — `Student` · `Homemaker` · `Retired` · `Military` ·
`Professional / office` · `Skilled trade` · `Driver / transport` ·
`Healthcare` · `Education` · `Self-employed` · `Other`

This is the one we are least sure of. These are plain-language labels
chosen for the visitor, not TurboRater's catalogue. If your mapping does
not recognise them they may all be falling through to `Other` — or worse,
going through unmapped and failing the enum.

**licenseType** — `U.S. driver’s license` · `Foreign driver’s license` ·
`International driver’s license` · `Learner permit` · `Matrícula consular` ·
`Passport` · `No license`

Note the **curly apostrophe** (U+2019) in the two `driver’s` values, and
the **accent** in `Matrícula`. A byte comparison against a straight
apostrophe will not match.

**maritalStatus** — `Single` · `Married` · `Divorced` · `Widowed` ·
`Separated`

**relationship** (driver 2 onward only; driver 1 never sends it) —
`Spouse` · `Child` · `Parent` · `Sibling` · `Other relative` · `Other`

**gender** — `Female` · `Male`

**residenceType** — the four values you gave us, sent as-is. Never
defaulted; the visitor must pick.

**biLimit** — `30/60/25` · `50/100/50` · `100/300/100` · `250/500/100`
(combined, PD in slot 3)

**comprehensive** / **collision** — `$250` · `$500` · `$1,000`, or the
literal `None` when the visitor chose liability-only.

**vehicles[].usage** — `Commuting to work` · `Pleasure` · `Business` ·
`Rideshare`

`Rideshare` is worth a look. Many carriers decline it outright, and if it
is not in the enum it fails the quote rather than the vehicle.

**annualMiles** — a number, converted from the visitor's band before
sending.

**licenseState** — all 50 USPS abbreviations, plus `Outside the U.S.` when
the document is not U.S.-issued. Only sent for a U.S. licence or learner
permit; cleared otherwise.

**state** (garaging) — `TX` or `NM` only.

**dob**, **priorExpiration**, **submittedAt** — ISO, unchanged.

## Fields the form deliberately never sends

Violations, accidents, tickets, SSN, licence images, payment details. If
TurboRater requires any of these to rate, that is a decision to make
together — it is not an oversight.

## How to reproduce without burning a carrier quote

The bridge is a pass-through, so a `curl` at `/public-quote` with the shape
above reaches the same code path. `docs/quote-api.md` in this repo has the
full payload as the browser emits it.
