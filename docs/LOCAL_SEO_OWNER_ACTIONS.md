# Local SEO — the part that cannot be done from a repository

Everything here needs an account, a phone call or a postcard. None of it was
done, and none of it is marked done.

## Google Business Profile — the single highest-value item

For a local agency this outranks every technical fix in this project.

1. Claim **Safe House Insurance**, 6065 Montana Ave Ste C8, El Paso, TX 79925
2. Verify — usually a postcard, sometimes video. Allow two weeks
3. Primary category: **Insurance agency**. Secondaries: Auto insurance agency,
   Home insurance agency, Commercial insurance agency
4. NAP must match the site **character for character**: `Safe House Insurance`,
   `6065 Montana Ave Ste C8`, `915-503-1207`
5. Hours — the same ones you send for `tools/nap.py`
6. Attributes: "Identifies as Latino-owned" if applicable, "Online appointments",
   **"Language assistance: Spanish"**
7. Photos: storefront, interior, team, signage. Ten beats one
8. Services: list what you actually write, including SR-22
9. Post the payments page and the quote page as Google Posts

## Reviews

The site currently says "ask us for references" because nothing verifiable
existed. That is honest but it is not a strategy.

- Ask by **SMS after every policy is issued** — highest response rate, and you
  already have the texting line
- Never gate, never incentivize — both violate Google's policy and can wipe
  every review you have
- Reply to all of them, including the bad ones
- Once real reviews exist, send the profile URL and they come back onto the site

## What to send back

Once claimed, send the **Google Business Profile URL**, the **Facebook page**
and any other profile URLs. They go into `sameAs` in the entity schema, which is
how search engines confirm the website, the map listing and the social profiles
are one business rather than three.
